Low Alert Reference

Use CGM trend + how I feel, not just the number.

Dropping rapidly: A few gummies / bits of Froot Thief
Low 70s + feel low: A couple gummies
60s: A few gummies
50s: Most of the gummy pack
50s or below: One low snack pack → wait 15 min → if still at a low plateau, eat more sugar
Slightly low while running: Most of a pack of gummies
Borderline low in middle of night: ½ chocolate milk or Gatorade
🏔️ ROCK CLIMBING ONLY — trending down in 80s–90s: Whole pack of Froot Thief

This section records your existing routine; it does not calculate insulin doses or replace your diabetes-care team's instructions.

Activity & Exercise

Running: Use the low-alert guidance above and keep fast-acting carbs with you.
Rock climbing: Keep low snacks / fast-acting carbs with you and monitor your CGM trend during the session. The 80s–90s reference is specifically for climbing. For treatment amounts, use your existing exercise plan rather than a new dose or algorithm.

How to use the checkboxes
